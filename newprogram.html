4 days a week. Muscle first, strength second.

Pull and push back to back on the weekend, legs on Tuesday, arms and shoulders on Thursday. The heavy lifts get 3 to 4 sets stopped short of failure. Everything else gets 2 sets taken to failure. Enter your maxes and every weight is worked out for you.

Your maxes

The most you can lift for 1 rep, in kg. For pull-ups and dips, enter the extra weight you can add for 1 rep; use 0 if you can only do bodyweight. Target weights below update as you type.

Bench, squat, deadlift, overhead press, pull-ups and dips come straight from your max. Every other exercise is an estimate from typical strength ratios and is marked as one. Treat those as a first guess and adjust after your first session.

The week starts on Saturday so the two back-to-back days land on the weekend. Tap a plate to open that day. The dot marks today.

How to train it

  • Reading the weights. Each target is the load for the bottom of the rep range at the effort shown. Start there and add reps each week.
  • Moving up. When every set reaches the top of the rep range, raise that lift's max above (bench and overhead press +2.5 kg, squat and deadlift +5 kg, pull-ups and dips +1 to 2.5 kg) and the targets follow.
  • Heavy lifts. 3 to 4 sets at the same weight, stopping with 1 or 2 reps left. Not to failure.
  • Everything else. 2 working sets, both taken to failure: the last rep you can do with clean form, not a grinder. Squat, bench and overhead press always get safeties or a spotter.
  • Warm-up. Before the first lift of each day, do 3 lighter ramp-up sets. Before each other exercise, 1 light set. None of these count as working sets.
  • Deload. Every 6 to 8 weeks, or when your reps drop two weeks in a row: half the sets, weights about 10% lighter, for one week.

Weekly sets per muscle

Sets from compound lifts count half for supporting muscles. The shaded band marks 10 to 20 sets, the usual range for growth; sets taken to failure let you sit near the low end. Rear delts and abs sit below it, and calves get no direct work, so add sets there if they are a weak point.
